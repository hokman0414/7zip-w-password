# Stakeholder and partnership log: The Jewish Federations of North America

As of 2026-11-06. Organization-level public records only. Member means a member federation, not a person. No individual donor, staff member, or private individual's location is recorded. "Shell" is used only if a filing shows an entity with no disclosed operations. None of the entities below met that test.

## Top organization

The Jewish Federations of North America, Inc. (EIN 13-1624240, New York, NTEE T700) is the national membership body. Its own site describes 141 federations, activity in more than 70 countries, and $2.3 billion distributed annually by the system. The national office is at 25 Broadway, 17th Floor, New York. Source: [Who We Are](https://www.jewishfederations.org/about-us/who-we-are).

## Member federations and their public presence

JFNA states the count as 141. This log does not list all 141 and does not list people. Three member federations have a current public corporate-partnership presence:

- Milwaukee Jewish Federation publishes a 2026 Corporate Partnership Program with tiers from $5,000 to $50,000. The same document lists JFNA, the Jewish Agency, and JDC as national and overseas partners. Source: [2026 program PDF](https://milwaukeejewish.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Corporate-Partnership-Program-2026.pdf).
- Jewish Federation of Southern New Jersey publishes community and corporate partners, including JFNA, the Claims Conference, ResinTech Inc., Freedom Mortgage, and Davis Enterprises. Source: [partners page](https://www.jewishsouthjersey.org/partners/).
- Jewish Federation of Greater MetroWest NJ posted a corporate-sponsorship manager role, updated 2026-08-24, to build a sponsorship program. Source: [JFNA job listing](https://www.jewishfederations.org/get-involved/explore-career-opportunities/career-opportunities/manager-corporate-sponsorship--development-1780056817).

## Policies and partnerships in those companies

EDGAR full-text search for "Jewish Federations of North America" and for "Jewish Federation" in S-1, F-1, and 424B4 filings from 2026-08-06 through 2026-11-06 returned no hits. The only EDGAR hit for the full name in that window is JFBP, LLC's own municipal-advisor annual amendment on 2026-08-19, which is not an IPO.

The companies named as corporate partners by the Southern New Jersey federation, ResinTech, Freedom Mortgage, and Davis Enterprises, are not in the IPO table.

JFNA's current policy agenda is health appropriations, homeland-security grants, the section 25F scholarship credit, and rebuilding Israel. Several new issuers are biotechnology companies, so they sit in the same broad health sector as one lobbying issue. That is not evidence of a partnership, a shared policy, or an investment. No sourced partnership between JFNA and any company in the table was found.
